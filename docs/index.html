Cardano TxWorkshop

A workbench for Cardano transactions. Open one, see exactly what it does, check it against the ledger's rules, change it, sign it and send it.

App Store — coming soon Mac download — coming soon Read the guide

On Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro. The Mac download adds a local cardano-node and cardano-cli, which the App Store doesn't allow.

What it does

Inspect

Inputs, outputs, assets, mints, certificates, votes, proposals, scripts and metadata, laid out plainly, with token names and links to your block explorer.

CBOR and CDDL

Every item tied to its bytes, each field named, non-canonical encoding flagged, and any item checked against the ledger's schema for its era.

Validate

The ledger's Phase 1 rules and Plutus script evaluation, offline, with budgets per redeemer, a script trace, and what-if runs.

Build

Outputs, mints, script inputs, certificates, withdrawals, votes and proposals. Addresses, pools and keys in any form, typed or from files.

Sign

Keys from a recovery phrase, kept in the Keychain behind Face ID or Touch ID, or a Ledger, Trezor or Keystone hardware wallet.

Submit

Through Blockfrost, Koios, Ogmios, Yaci DevKit, or, on the Mac download, your own cardano-node or cardano-cli. Then track confirmation.

Yours, on your device

No account and no analytics. Documents stay where you save them; API keys and signing keys stay in the Keychain and are never written to a document. See the privacy policy.
