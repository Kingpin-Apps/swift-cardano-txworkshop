• Choose when a transaction is valid by time, not slot: "From now", "Until tomorrow", "A week" and more. Build shows the slots it works out and warns when an interval has already closed.
• Adding a source address fetches its UTxOs on its own, along with the chain data when it is missing or from an earlier epoch.
